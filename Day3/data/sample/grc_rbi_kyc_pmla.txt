RBI Master Direction (Illustrative) on KYC

Section 1. Scope
This Master Direction is issued by the RBI under the powers granted by the PMLA and the rules made under it. It applies to all regulated entities.

Section 2. Customer due diligence
Regulated entities shall carry out KYC at onboarding and shall identify the Beneficial Owner of every legal-entity customer. Enhanced due diligence is required for high-risk customers. Periodic KYC updation is required at intervals determined by risk category.

Section 3. Reporting
A Suspicious Transaction Report must be furnished to FIU-IND within seven days of the principal officer concluding that a transaction is suspicious, as required under Section 12 of the PMLA.

Section 4. Penalties
Non-compliance with this Master Direction may attract monetary penalty imposed by the RBI. Failure to report under Section 12 of the PMLA attracts a penalty under Section 13 of the PMLA, and the Director of FIU-IND may recommend further action.

Section 5. Record keeping
Records of identity and transactions shall be maintained for five years, as required by Section 12 of the PMLA.
