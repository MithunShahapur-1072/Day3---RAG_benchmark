Cross-border Acquisition Note (Illustrative): FEMA and Valuation

Section 1. Foreign Direct Investment
An acquisition of shares of an Indian company by a non-resident is Foreign Direct Investment under FEMA and must comply with applicable pricing guidelines. The price shall not be lower than the fair value determined by a Registered Valuer.

Section 2. Valuation under Companies Act 2013
The Companies Act 2013 requires valuation of shares and assets by a Registered Valuer in prescribed cases. The Valuation Report must state the methodology and assumptions.

Section 3. Reporting
The Indian investee must report the inflow to the RBI within the prescribed time. Delay is a contravention under FEMA that may be compounded on payment of a compounding fee.

Section 4. FCRA
A recipient of foreign contribution that is governed by FCRA shall not be treated as an investment vehicle for FDI purposes.
