Governance Control Mapping (Illustrative): BRSR and ISO 31000

Section 1. BRSR disclosures
SEBI requires listed entities to publish the BRSR annually. The BRSR core disclosures cover environmental, social and governance indicators and must be supported by internal controls.

Section 2. Risk management framework
ISO 31000 provides principles for risk management: risk identification, analysis, evaluation and treatment. The risk register maintained under ISO 31000 shall be mapped to each BRSR disclosure that depends on it.

Section 3. Internal Audit
Internal Audit shall test the controls supporting BRSR data at least annually and report exceptions to the audit committee. A control that cannot be mapped to a BRSR obligation is flagged as unmapped.

Section 4. Penalty exposure
Misstatement in the BRSR may lead to enforcement action by SEBI against the listed entity.
